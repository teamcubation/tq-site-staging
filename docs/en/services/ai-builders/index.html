AI Builders: train internal specialists in automation with AI.

The capability to automate, inside your company.

AI Builders is Teamcubation's program that trains technical profiles to design and build complex automations with AI, agents and integrations between systems. It lasts four months full-time, for up to three participants who learn on concrete needs of their company.

Schedule a conversation
Duration
Four months
Participants
Up to 3 people
Modality
Full-time squad

Who is AI Builders for?

Companies that want to develop internal specialists in automation with AI. The program is aimed at IT profiles or developers in training who can dedicate their full time to the journey.

What does complex automation mean?

Processes that connect several systems, incorporate business logic or combine agents, integrations and workflows. Participants learn by building on company needs, with expert support.

How does AI Builders work?

  1. 1. Choose company processes.

    Needs that require automation and integration between systems are identified.

  2. 2. Build with support.

    Participants work with mentors on tools like n8n, Copilot Studio, Claude Cowork and the AI models' interfaces.

  3. 3. Develop your own capability.

    The journey aims for participants to understand what they build and be able to sustain it, expand it and tackle new needs.

Specialists who know their processes.

The program works to bring complex automations to production and train the people who can sustain them. Experience is built within your company's processes, with your needs and integrations.

How do we track progress?

In AI Builders, Teamboarding lets you follow participants' progress and the work on the program's automations, with reports for those responsible.

Frequently asked questions.

What profile does the participant need?

An IT profile or a developer in training, with a technical foundation and availability to dedicate four full months to the program.

How is it different from Booster AI?

Booster AI works with business teams on processes of their own role, over five weeks. AI Builders trains technical profiles for complex automations and integrations, over four months full-time.

Do you work on exercises or on the company's processes?

On the company's automations, chosen based on its needs. Participants learn while building with expert mentors.

What capability does AI Builders aim to leave?

People able to design, build and evolve automations with AI within the company, with experience on their own processes.
