Incubation: software development with developer training.

The software you need today, and the team that can sustain it tomorrow.

Incubation is Teamcubation's program in which a mixed team builds software while training developers on your company's project. The program lasts six months. At the end, the developers can join your team knowing the product, the code and the business.

Duration
Six months
Format
Mixed Teamcubation team
Focus
Software and developer training

Who is Incubation for?

Companies that need to build software and grow their technical team. Incubation combines both needs in a project where developers train on the product they are building.

How is talent trained within the project?

Experienced Teamcubation developers guide the people in training while they work on the company's software. Technical experience is built alongside knowledge of the product, the code and the business.

How does Incubation work?

  1. 1. Work on the product.

    The mixed team builds the software the company needs during the program.

  2. 2. Train within development.

    The people in training work with experienced leadership and engineering practices.

  3. 3. Prepare for continuity.

    At the end, the developers can join the company with experience on the project they helped build.

Software and people who know how to sustain it.

The program combines software, documentation and work practices with developers trained on the product itself. Their possible joining of the team is defined within the framework of the program.

How do we track progress?

In Incubation, Teamboarding supports the measurement of the developers-in-training's progress during the project.

Frequently asked questions.

Who guides the development?

Experienced Teamcubation developers support the team in training and the project's engineering practices.

What happens with the developers at the end?

They can join the company's team. They arrive with experience on the product, the code and the business they worked on during the program.

What sets the Incubation format apart?

Software development and developer training happen within the same project. The program works on the product and on the capability of the people who can sustain it.

How long is Incubation?

Six months, with a mixed team that builds software and trains developers on the company's project.
