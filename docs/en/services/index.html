Services

AI and talent-development programs for your company.

Teamcubation offers eight services to identify AI opportunities, train business teams, develop internal specialists and build software while training developers. The starting point depends on your needs and your teams' experience.

Schedule a conversation

What can we achieve together?

Have the people in your company use AI to work more efficiently.

Surface layer — business teams. Your teams know the tasks they need to improve. Training starts from that knowledge and works on their tools and processes.

If you need: Use AI in daily tasks

Starter AI

Teams with no prior experience learn to use AI on their tasks with the tools they already have.

For whom: Non-technical teams, up to 16 people
Duration: One month
Focus: Applying tools to your own tasks

If you need: Automate your team's processes

Booster AI

Business teams automate their own processes with AI and agents, guided by expert mentors.

For whom: Business teams, up to 8 people
Duration: Five weeks plus two months of follow-up
Focus: Automation with AI and agents

If you need: Try a focused pilot

Booster AI Intensive

Six people in three pairs build a first real automation per pair, in a focused pilot.

For whom: Six people in three pairs
Duration: One week
Focus: A first real automation per pair

Have your most advanced AI users build technology.

Middle layer — building with AI. Business profiles can create internal tools. Technical profiles can go deeper into integrations and more complex processes.

If you need: Create internal applications

VibeCode Studio

Teams already using AI build business micro-apps, with no traditional programming.

For whom: Teams that already adopted AI, up to 8 people
Duration: Two months
Focus: Business micro-apps

If you need: Build technical automation capability

AI Builders

Technical profiles train to build complex automations and integrations between systems within your company.

For whom: Technical profiles, up to 3 people
Duration: Four months, full-time
Focus: Complex automations and integrations

Add talent and new tech skills to your development team.

IT core — development teams. We train development teams to adopt AI and we train developers while they build the software the company needs.

If you need: Bring AI into development

Booster AI Dev

Development teams adopt AI tools and agents on their sprint and repositories.

For whom: Development teams, up to 8 people
Duration: One month
Focus: Adoption on the real sprint

If you need: Build software and train developers

Incubation

Software built while training developers who can then join your team.

For whom: Your organization and a mixed Teamcubation team
Duration: Six months
Focus: Software and training on the project

The first step depends on your starting point.

Already using AI and want to automate processes?
