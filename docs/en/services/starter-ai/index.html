Starter AI: AI applied to the work of non-technical teams.

The AI your team already has, applied to everyday tasks.

Starter AI is Teamcubation's program for teams with no prior experience in AI. Over one month, participants learn to use their company's tools to write, analyze information and prepare reports on their own tasks, with an expert mentor by their side.

Schedule a conversation
Duration
One month
Participants
Up to 16 people
Profile
Non-technical teams

Who is Starter AI for?

Human resources, finance, operations, marketing, sales or administration teams that need to start using AI in their work. The program starts from the tasks each person knows and performs.

Which tasks does it work on?

Writing and summarizing documents, analyzing information, preparing reports or organizing work materials. Tasks are chosen based on the team's needs and the tools available at the company.

How does Starter AI work?

  1. 1. Recognize the tasks.

    Together with the team, we identify where AI can be applied in their everyday work.

  2. 2. Build a shared foundation.

    Charlas AI provides the program's starting point and supports leaders in guiding their teams with these new capabilities.

  3. 3. Practice with a guide.

    Each person works on their tasks with expert support. Learning happens while they use the tools.

  4. 4. Review progress.

    The journey is recorded in Teamboarding to support progress and track what the team takes on.

A team with experience to use AI.

The goal is for people to bring AI into their tasks and understand how to apply the tools on their own. That experience is the foundation for moving toward automating full processes.

How do we track progress?

In Starter AI, Teamboarding records people's progress and supports the training's follow-up. Those responsible for the program receive progress reports.

Frequently asked questions.

Do you need to know how to code?

No. Starter AI is designed for non-technical teams with no prior experience in AI.

Which tools are used?

Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT, depending on your company's tools and licensing. The selection is defined based on the team's tasks.

How long is it and how many people take part?

Starter AI lasts one month and is aimed at teams of up to 16 people.

How does it relate to a previous training course?

A training course can provide a useful foundation. Starter AI brings the practice to each person's tasks, with a guide who supports its application.

What could the next step be?

When the team already uses AI and needs to automate full processes, Booster AI lets you work toward that goal. The path is defined based on the company's need.
